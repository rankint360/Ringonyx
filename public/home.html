RELIABILITY

What happens when a call goes off script?

It goes unanswered

Missed calls generate no charge in the prototype. Live fallback routing is planned.

The completion arrives twice

Replaying the same completion leaves a single charge in the ledger.

The renter changes mid-call

The original assignment stays attached to that call. Future calls use the new renter.

FAQ

Before you try it.

Can I forward live calls today?

The current prototype simulates inbound calls and completion events. Live carrier forwarding is planned.

What happens when I replace a renter?

New calls use the new assignment. Earlier calls keep their original renter and rate.

Does Ringonyx issue invoices?

The prototype records qualified charges and shows them in the portal. Invoice generation and payments are planned.

Can I bring an existing tracking number?

Number porting is planned. The prototype accepts sample tracking numbers for simulation.

Does Ringonyx supply buyers or publishers?

The network roadmap is designed around the buyers and publishers you bring.

Can I return calls from my browser?

Try the sample dialer in Numbers and Calling. Live callbacks are planned.

What’s on the roadmap?

Live telephony, number provisioning, dynamic number insertion, buyer and publisher portals, bidding, web lead delivery, and conversation intelligence.
